## DETAILED DAY‑BY‑DAY PLAN  

### **Day 1 – Arrival in New York City**  
- **Flight** – Book a *direct* Manila → New York (JFK) flight on **Singapore Airlines** (or Qatar Airways with one stop in Doha). Aim for a morning departure (≈ 15 h total) so you land in the early afternoon local time.  
- **Transport to hotel** – Take the **AirTrain JFK** to **Jamaica Station**, then the **E subway** to **5th Ave/53rd St** (≈ 45 min).  
- **Check‑in** – **The Paul Hotel** (mid‑midtown, boutique, great location for Times Square & Broadway).  
- **Afternoon** – Stroll through **Times Square**, explore the flagship stores, and soak up the neon energy.  
- **Evening** – Grab a quick bite at **Los Tacos No. 1** (Chelsea Market) and then catch a **Broadway show** (e.g., *Hamilton* or *The Lion King*).  
- **Nightcap** – Head up to **230 Fifth Rooftop Bar** for skyline views.

---

### **Day 2 – Classic NYC Highlights**  
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 08:00 | Breakfast at **Ess-a-Bagel** (classic NY bagel). |
| 09:00 | **Statue of Liberty & Ellis Island** – take the early ferry from Battery Park (reserve tickets online). |
| 12:30 | Lunch in **Financial District** – try **Fraunces Tavern** (historic). |
| 14:00 | **9/11 Memorial & Museum** (allow 2 h). |
| 16:30 | Walk across the **Brooklyn Bridge**; stop for a coffee at **Brooklyn Bridge Park**. |
| 18:30 | Return to Manhattan – explore **Central Park** (rent a bike or take a carriage ride). |
| 20:00 | Dinner in **Hell’s Kitchen** – **5 Napkin Burger** or **Totto Ramen**. |
| 22:00 | Optional jazz at **Blue Note** (Greenwich Village). |

---

### **Day 3 – Travel to Washington, DC**  

**Morning**  
- **Check‑out** and store luggage at the hotel.  
- Grab a quick pastry at **Le Pain Quotidien**.  

**Travel**  
- Take **Amtrak Acela Express** from **Penn Station** to **Washington Union Station** (≈ 3 h, business class if you want extra comfort).  
- Arrive around **12:00 pm**, drop bags at **Hotel Harrington** (central, budget‑friendly, steps from the National Mall).  

**Afternoon**  
- **National Mall** – walk from the **Washington Monument** to the **Lincoln Memorial** (stop at the **World War II Memorial**).  
- **Smithsonian National Museum of American History** (free).  

**Evening**  
- Dinner in **Penn Quarter** at **Old Ebbitt Grill** (historic, near the White House).  
- Night stroll to see the **Monuments illuminated** (great photo ops).

| 11:30 | **Library of Congress** (stunning architecture). |
| 13:00 | Lunch at **Ben’s Chili Bowl** (famous half‑smoke). |

### **Day 5 – Morning Flight to Boston & First Impressions**  

**Morning**  
- Early checkout, store luggage at the hotel.  
- Take a **taxi/Uber** to **Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport** (or Dulles, depending on flight).  

**Flight** – **Direct** Manila → Boston (BOS) isn’t available; the quickest route is **Manila → New York (JFK) → Boston (BOS)**. Book a **same‑day connection** (≈ 2 h) on **Delta** or **American Airlines**.  

**Arrival in Boston** – 12:30 pm local time.  

**Transport** – Take the **Silver Line** to **South Station**, then a short walk to **The Godfrey Hotel Boston** (stylish, downtown, close to the Freedom Trail).  

**Afternoon**  
- Quick lunch at **Quincy Market** (clam chowder in a bread bowl).  
- Begin the **Freedom Trail** walk (starts at Boston Common, ends at Bunker Hill). Highlights: **Massachusetts State House**, **Paul Revere House**, **Old North Church**.  

**Evening**  
- Dinner at **Legal Sea Foods** (iconic New England seafood).  
- Sunset stroll along the **Boston Harborwalk**; optional harbor cruise (30 min).  

---

### **Day 6 – Day‑Trip Options (Choose One)**  

**Option A – Salem (witch‑history)**  
- **Transport** – Take the **MBTA commuter rail** from **North Station** to **Salem** (≈ 30 min).  
- **Highlights** – **Salem Witch Museum**, **The House of the Seven Gables**, **Peabody Essex Museum**.  
- **Lunch** – **Turner’s Seafood** (fresh oysters).  
- Return to Boston by early evening.

**Option B – Harvard & Cambridge**  
- **Transport** – Subway Red Line to **Harvard Square**.  
- **Highlights** – **Harvard Yard** tour, **Harvard Art Museums**, **MIT Campus** (Stata Center).  
- **Lunch** – **Mr. Bartley’s Burger** (famous campus burger joint).  
- Return to Boston for a relaxed dinner at **The Friendly Toast** (retro‑vibe brunch‑all‑day).

**Evening (both options)**  
- Nightcap at **The Beehive** (live music, eclectic menu).  

---

### **Day 7 – Last Hours in Boston & Departure**  

- **Morning** – Pack, check‑out, store luggage at the hotel.  
- **Breakfast** – **Tatte Bakery** (pastries + coffee).  
- **Last‑minute shopping** – **Newbury Street** (designer boutiques, local shops).  
- **Lunch** – Light bite at **Sam LaGrassa’s** (famous sandwich shop).  

- **Transport to Airport** – **Silver Line** to **Logan International Airport** (arrive 2 h before departure).  

- **Flight home** – Connect back through **JFK** or **LAX** to Manila (choose the same carrier you used inbound for smoother baggage handling).
